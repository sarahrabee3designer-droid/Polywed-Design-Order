What is included

1 · Brand identity & guidelines

  • Built on your questionnaire answers: simple, young and proudly Saudi, a value line from a trusted Saudi maker
  • 2 logo directions (symbol + name, leading with "UB"), then 1 refined into the final logo
  • English and Arabic (معاً نبني) lockups, plus single-color and reversed versions
  • Color palette led by purple, with no blue, orange or Polywed teal/green, in print and screen codes
  • English and Arabic typography, with equal weight for both languages
  • Packaging system so the next 3–5 UB products follow one look (layout, naming and color coding)
  • Essential brand guidelines book, about 12 to 16 pages (PDF)
  • Final logo files: AI, EPS, SVG, PDF and PNG

2 & 3 · Packaging masters

  • One master design per format: plastic pail and paper bag
  • Bilingual artwork using the product details and technical text Polywed supplies
  • Artwork set up on the printer's die-line or template
  • Print-ready PDF and editable source file for each
  • One realistic mock-up of each pack for approvals and marketing

Revisions

  • 2 revision rounds on each deliverable are included
  • Please send one combined set of comments per round from your approval team
  • Extra rounds, or changes after final approval: 50 SAR per round
