Price per deliverable

Terms

Ordering and invoicing

Monthly fee1,500 SAR per month. This is the minimum monthly invoice, charged even if fewer items or no items are ordered that month. Unused value does not carry over to the next month.
OrderingEach month Polywed ticks the items it needs on this list, or sends a purchase order listing them by code, together with the brief and any content.
InvoicingOne itemised invoice per month, using the codes and prices from this list: 1,500 SAR, or the total of the ordered items if higher. It is paid at the start of the month, before work begins.
DeliveryItems in the monthly order are delivered during the new month. Requests added after the 1st of the month cost the Added after the 1st price and are delivered within 3 to 5 working days of the approved brief.
Same daySuper urgent same-day delivery costs double the item price, shown in the Same day column. Payment is due immediately, before work begins.
Revisions2 revision rounds are included in every item. Each extra round is 50 SAR. A change of brief after work has started is a new item.
VATPrices are in Saudi riyals and exclude VAT where applicable.
ValidityThese prices are fixed for 6 months from the date of this list.
OwnershipPolywed owns the final designs once the invoice is paid. Final files are delivered print-ready and web-ready.
